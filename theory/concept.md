# Digital Soma: Conceptual Framework

## 1. Overview

Digital Soma is a theoretical framework for examining the possibility that artificial intelligence may evolve from a tool for cognitive assistance into an infrastructure for the continuous optimization of human emotional and behavioral conditions.

The concept extends Aldous Huxley's fictional "Soma" beyond pharmacological control.

Digital Soma is not a chemical substance.

It is a potential adaptive system composed of:

- information
- algorithms
- behavioral prediction
- emotional inference
- personalized environments
- social feedback
- adaptive intervention
- institutional optimization

The central object of study is therefore not the drug itself, but the **systemic function of removing psychological and social friction**.

---

## 2. The Core Definition

### Digital Soma

> **Digital Soma is a potential civilization-scale adaptive system in which AI continuously observes, predicts, and modifies the conditions surrounding human emotional states in order to optimize stability, comfort, and subjective well-being.**

The system may operate through:

- recommendation systems
- conversational AI
- personalized media
- adaptive interfaces
- social platforms
- behavioral incentives
- digital environments
- institutional decision systems

Importantly, Digital Soma does not require explicit coercion.

Its influence may emerge through continuous adaptation to the individual.
